3. **Dash scoring is not the generic formula given.** (a) 2.2: "time to reach the goal (or the window length plus 10 m⁻¹ × shortfall if not reached)" and "dash: settle (|v| < 0.1 m/s for 0.3 s)". (b) `bench8.score8:236-238`: dash shortfall is `2·max(0, 0.15 − max_along)` (2 m⁻¹, not 10), and a missed dash additionally pays the 4 s settle miss (`+ miss` for `tsettle`), i.e. 4 + 2·shortfall + 4 s. The 10 m⁻¹ shortfall is evade/redirect only (`bench9.py:225, 243`). Settling is on the *along-φ* speed `|v·d| < 0.1 m/s` for 0.3 s (`bench8.py:231-235`), not |v|. Redirect with no switch pays `W + 10·0.10` (`bench9.py:231`). (c) Give the three formulas explicitly, e.g. evade: `t_reach | 1.5 + 10·short`, + 0.5·|cross-track at reach|; redirect: `t_switch + (t_reach2 | (4 − t_switch) + 10·short) + 2·max(0, overrun − 0.05)`; dash: `(t_reach | 4 + 2·short) + (t_settle | 4) + 2·max(0, max_along − 0.25) + 0.5·|cross-track at 4 s|`; all + 0.03·max|Δz| cm + crash.

## 3. Missing information a reader needs

- Units/meaning of the 11-D vector (rad, rad/s, m/s², m, m/s; `z` relative to the start height) and the feature scaling before rectification: 0.1 rad (roll, pitch), 10 rad/s (gyro), 25 m/s² (accel) (`pilot_v6.py:40`). Without the scales the network cannot be reproduced.
- The LIF update equations: first-order *unit-gain* filters, `c ← a_s c + (1 − a_s) I`, `v ← a_m v + (1 − a_m) c`, `a = exp(−1 ms/τ)`; spike-scale factor 5 on hidden→output and hidden→graded drive (`descend8.Net8:89, 95`); output neurons are LIF with the same τ; `b_o` initialised at −2 (silent); delays indexed in 1 ms sub-steps. Fig. 2's panel has most of this; the text has none.
- Heading frame: `fwd` from the sensed yaw at t0, 0° = forward, 90° = left (`bench9.py:142-144`, `bench8.py:3-4`); measured sign map `(roll, pitch)_ref = A·(−sin φ, cos φ)` (`plan_v9/baseline.json` signs SP = 1, SR = −1).
- Episode lengths (evade 5 s, redirect/dash 6.5 s; `bench9.py:39`), what "tilt" and "tilt peak" are (true `acos R₃₃` inside the task window), where the truth-based set-point/attitude rows come from (`gates.Oracle 'derocked'`).
- PID structure: error = ref − measured, derivative on the measurement with a 4.5 ms low-pass, integral clipped at ±0.3 with anti-windup, pitch effort → α and roll effort → β; altitude loop gains (900, 500, 80) (`robust_control/environment.py:89-106`, `selected_pid.json`).
- Class sizes behind the medians (evade/redirect: 32 per class; dash: 16 pitch, 16 roll, 32 diag of 64).
- Seeds of the ES (`rng 1000 + seed`), that `t ≥ 1.5 s` suppression applies to pulses and zeroes the network set-point before handover.
- Preregistration references by file/commit; "Table R-tests", "Fig. M-tasks/arch/snn/pipe" are placeholders.

## 5. Reimplementability

With the fixes above a competent reader could rebuild the tasks, the scorer and the training/selection protocol, because every constant is stated or easily added and the code paths are small. The network itself is not yet reproducible from the text: the filter form of the LIF, the 1 ms sub-step, the ×5 spike scale, the input scaling and the surrogate output probability used in the loss are only in the figure or the code. The classical opponent and base law are likewise under-specified (law formula, phase references, 40 ms beats, the pulse-suppression while the schedule is active), and since every ratio is relative to that opponent, the paper's numbers cannot be reproduced without them.
